Credits and licences

Who made what, and what you may do with it.

This studio stands on other people's work. This page says whose, under which licence, and what you must do if you use it in a published video.

Short version

Check the Remotion licence for your company size. Credit the bundled music if you use it. Prompts found on the web belong to their authors. Everything vendored keeps its original licence.

This repository

The repository itself has no licence file yet. That means default copyright applies: the original work here is not offered for reuse, copying or redistribution. You may read it, and clone it for your own private use. If you want to reuse something, ask first. The third-party material listed below keeps its own licence and is not affected by this.

This website is generated from the repository by a script in docs/_build. It quotes and links to the sources below rather than copying them.

Remotion

Videos here are rendered with Remotion. Remotion is free for individuals and for companies with up to 3 employees. Larger companies need a company licence. Check the current terms at remotion.dev/license before you use it commercially, because they can change.

Official Remotion agent skills

The official Remotion agent skills are not redistributed in this repo, because their upstream repository declares no licence. Install them yourself from the studio/ folder:

npx remotion skills add

The versions are pinned in studio/skills-lock.json.

brag (third party, MIT)

third_party/brag/ is a byte-for-byte copy of latent-spaces/brag by Shunit Haviv Hakimi, pinned to one upstream commit, under the MIT licence. The licence text sits next to the files. It is not edited here.

Credit the music

The music bundled with brag, "Happy Beats / Business Moves", is by Sascha Ende (ende.app) and is licensed CC BY 4.0, not MIT. If a published video uses it, you must credit it. A line you can paste:

Music: Sascha Ende, ende.app (CC BY 4.0)

The sound effects bundled with brag come from Kenney.nl and unicae_games and are CC0, so no credit is required.

video-shotcraft (third party, Apache-2.0)

third_party/video-shotcraft/ is an unmodified subset of video-shotcraft by Vincent Wei, pinned to one commit, under the Apache License 2.0. The licence and a NOTICE file are kept with it. The cards and demo source are Vincent Wei's work.

The shot library on this site is a separate work, written in our own words, that points back to each upstream card and credits it.

Prompts from other authors

The prompt-motion-search skill looks up animations on prompt-motion.com. The prompts and videos it finds belong to their authors. The skill always shows the author and the link, and saves a chosen prompt only with credit, on demand. If you reuse a prompt, keep the credit.

The research gallery

The gallery and the research findings collect videos made with Claude by other people, from GitHub, Reddit, X, YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and the web. The policy is simple:

  • Entries are paraphrased in our own words, never copied wholesale.
  • Every entry links to its source and names its creator.
  • Nobody watched the videos while researching. Descriptions come from captions, READMEs, post text and articles, and entries are marked verified or unconfirmed.
  • The videos themselves are not hosted here. They stay with their creators.

If you made something listed there and want it changed or removed, open an issue on the repository.

Fonts

Videos use Inter Tight for text and JetBrains Mono for labels and code, loaded through Remotion's Google Fonts package. Both are under the SIL Open Font Licence 1.1, which allows use in published work. This website uses the Inter and JetBrains Mono families if you have them installed, and falls back to system fonts otherwise.

Sound effects in the studio

The sound cues in studio/public/sfx/ are generated with ffmpeg by a script in the repo, so they have no licence questions.
